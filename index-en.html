Texbook

A text, PDF, and comic viewer for Android

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 简体中文

Features

  • Read text (txt) files, PDFs, image sets (ZIP), and image files.
  • Texbook remembers where you left off and supports bookmarks and favorites.
  • Adjust text size, line spacing, font, background color, and theme.
  • Listen with read aloud, or let the text scroll automatically.
  • Comic viewing: page by page, continuous vertical scrolling, and two-page spreads.
  • Home screen widgets let you jump straight back into what you were reading.
  • Available in Korean, English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese.
  • No sign-up. Your reading history and settings stay on your device.

Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, you can pick files stored in your Drive and download them to your device to read. Texbook uses view-only permission and never modifies or deletes files in your Drive. File lists and contents are used only on your device and are not sent to the operator or any third party.

Ads and purchases

Ads (Google AdMob) are shown at the bottom of the app. A one-time Remove ads purchase turns them off. Purchases are processed by Google Play.
