Texbook Privacy Policy

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 简体中文

1. General

h.9com (the "Operator") values the privacy of Texbook users and complies with applicable privacy laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. This Privacy Policy explains how Texbook handles user information.

2. Personal information we collect

Texbook does not itself collect your personal information or send it to the Operator. The Operator runs no servers for Texbook.

  • There is no sign-up or login, and Texbook never asks for identifying information such as your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Texbook does not collect location information.
  • The names of files you open, reading positions, bookmarks, favorites, reading history, and display settings are stored only on your device.
  • Files downloaded from Google Drive, files extracted from ZIPs, files received from other apps, and cover images are also stored only on your device.

This data is not transmitted anywhere and is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also delete it yourself under Settings > Downloaded files and Trash.

However, the advertising and purchase features use Google services, and Google processes some information in the course of providing them. See section 5 for details.

3. Device permissions

Texbook uses the following permissions and access to provide its features. Information accessed this way is used only on your device; the Operator does not collect it or transmit it anywhere.

  • Files and folders: Texbook reads only the files you pick yourself and the folders you add with Add folder. It only reads them and never modifies or deletes them.
  • Notifications: used to show that Read aloud is playing and to let you stop it.
  • Internet: used to show ads, verify the Remove ads purchase, and connect to Google Drive.

You can revoke the notification permission at any time in your device settings.

4. Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, Texbook shows the list of files in your Drive and downloads the files you choose to your device. It uses view-only permission and never modifies or deletes files in your Drive.

File lists and contents are used only on your device and are not sent to the Operator or any third party. Texbook stores on your device only which Google Account you connected (the account's email address); it does not store passwords or access tokens.

You can disconnect at any time with Disconnect in Settings, and you can remove the permission granted to Texbook under Security in your Google Account settings.

Texbook's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Sharing with third parties and external services

The Operator does not provide your personal information to third parties or entrust its processing to others. However, Texbook includes the Google services listed below, and information processed by those services is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Advertising (Google AdMob): In the course of showing ads, Google may collect and use the advertising ID, device and app information, and IP address with the approximate location inferred from it. If you don't want personalized ads, you can delete your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your device's Settings > Google > Ads. If you purchase Remove ads, Texbook no longer loads ads.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing): The Remove ads purchase is processed by Google Play. Texbook does not receive payment details such as card numbers; it only checks through Google Play whether the purchase was made.
  • Read aloud: Texbook uses the text-to-speech (TTS) feature installed on your device. The text to be read is passed to that feature; Texbook does not store or transmit it separately.

Texbook contains no tools for usage analytics or crash reporting.

6. Children's privacy

Texbook does not itself collect personal information from any user, including children under 14.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes because of changes to the app's features or to the law, the updated policy and its effective date will be posted on this screen and on the web page. If Texbook ever begins to collect personal information, the policy will be revised and users notified before any collection starts.
